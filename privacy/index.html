Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

Open Meadow Games ("we", "us") is a small indie game studio in British Columbia, Canada. This policy explains what information we collect through our website (openmeadowgames.com) and through our internal social media publishing tool, and how we use it.

Our social media publishing tool

We use a private, self-hosted tool to schedule and publish our own studio content (such as game trailers and development videos) to our own accounts on platforms like TikTok, Instagram, Threads, Bluesky and YouTube. The tool is used only by Open Meadow Games and is not offered to the public.

When we connect one of our accounts (for example, through Login with TikTok), the platform shares with us:

  • Basic account information, such as the account's display name, username and profile picture.
  • Access tokens that let the tool upload and publish videos to that account on our behalf.

We use this information only to publish our own content to our own accounts and to show which account a post is going to. We do not collect information about other TikTok users, we do not sell or share any of this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

This information is stored on a private server that we control. Access tokens are deleted when we disconnect an account. You can revoke the tool's access at any time from your TikTok app settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services).

Our website

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages they view. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects information such as your approximate location, browser and device type. We use this only to improve the site. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

If you email us, we keep your message and email address only to reply to you.

Children

Our tools and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights and data deletion

You can ask us to see, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you by emailing openmeadowgames@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
